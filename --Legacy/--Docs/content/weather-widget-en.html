Current Weather Display

The widget displays current weather conditions with dynamic icons that change based on the current time and weather state.

Temperature

Shows the current temperature in Celsius, prominently displayed for quick reference.

Weather Condition

Displays a brief description of current weather conditions (e.g., "Light Rain", "Clear Sky", "Broken Clouds").

Dynamic Icons

Weather icons automatically change based on the current time (day/night) and weather conditions, providing accurate visual representation.

  • Icons adapt to time of day (sun for day, moon for night)
  • Weather-specific icons (rain, clouds, sun, etc.)
  • Automatic updates as conditions change

Location Display

Shows the detected location name (e.g., "Kafr ash Shaykh") with a location pin icon, confirming the auto-detection feature.

Medical Indicators for Ophthalmology

The Weather Widget includes specialized medical indicators designed specifically for ophthalmology doctors, providing valuable environmental health information.

Pollen Index

Displays the current pollen level as a percentage, helping doctors advise patients with allergies or eye sensitivity.

  • Shows pollen level percentage (e.g., 55%, 70%, 85%)
  • Color-coded progress bar (green, orange, red) indicating severity
  • Updated daily based on environmental conditions
  • Helps doctors recommend appropriate precautions

Dry Eye Risk

Indicates the risk level for dry eye conditions based on weather factors, helping doctors provide better patient care.

  • Shows dry eye risk percentage (e.g., 33%, 48%)
  • Color-coded progress bar (green for low, orange for moderate, red for high)
  • Calculated based on humidity, wind, and temperature factors
  • Helps doctors advise patients on eye care during specific weather conditions

Forecast Indicators

Both indicators are available for each day in the 4-day forecast, allowing doctors to plan ahead and inform patients about upcoming conditions.

  • Pollen and Dry Eye indicators shown for each forecast day
  • Color coding helps identify high-risk days
  • Enables proactive patient communication
Medical Reference:

These indicators are specifically designed for ophthalmology doctors to reference when treating patients. High pollen levels may affect patients with allergies, while high dry eye risk may require additional patient care recommendations during specific weather conditions.
