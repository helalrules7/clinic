Secretary Role

Front-Office Operations and Administrative Management

Administrative Front Office Booking Management

Role Overview

The Secretary role is designed for administrative staff to handle front-office operations, including appointment scheduling, patient registration, payment processing, and booking management.

Access Level: Administrative and front-office operations
Primary Responsibilities: Booking management, patient registration, payment processing, invoice management
Restrictions: Cannot create prescriptions or access medical consultations

Booking Workflow

Typical workflow for creating and managing bookings.

1

Search or Register Patient

Search for existing patient or register a new patient if they don't exist in the system.

Navigation 
Patients → Search
or
Patients → New Patient
/secretary/patients/new
2

Create Booking

Create a new booking/appointment for the patient with doctor and time selection.

Navigation 
Bookings → New Booking
POST /secretary/bookings
3

Process Payment

Record payment for the booking (booking fee, consultation fee, etc.).

Navigation 
Payments → Record Payment
/secretary/payments
4

Confirm Attendance

When patient arrives, confirm their attendance in the system.

API 
POST /secretary/bookings/{id}/confirm
5

Print Booking Details

Print booking confirmation and details for the patient.

Route 
/secretary/bookings/{id}/print

Payment Types & Methods

Available payment types and methods in the system.

Payment Types

  • Booking - Initial booking fee
  • Consultation - Consultation fee
  • Follow Up - Follow-up visit fee
  • Procedure - Medical procedure fee
  • Other - Other payment types

Payment Methods

  • Cash - Cash payment
  • Card - Credit/Debit card
  • Wallet - Patient wallet balance
  • Transfer - Bank transfer

Default Fees

  • Booking Fee: 50 EGP
  • Consultation Fee: 200 EGP
  • Follow-up Fee: 150 EGP
  • Procedure Fee: 500 EGP

Permissions & Access

Detailed permissions and access levels for the Secretary role.

Full Access

  • Create and manage bookings
  • Register new patients
  • Process payments
  • View invoices
  • Search patients
  • View booking details
  • Print booking confirmations

View Only

  • Patient medical records (view only)
  • Consultation history (view only)
  • Prescription history (view only)

Restricted

  • Create prescriptions (no access)
  • Edit consultations (no access)
  • User management (no access)
  • System settings (no access)
  • Backup & restore (no access)
