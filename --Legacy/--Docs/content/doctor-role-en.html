Role Overview

The Doctor role is the primary medical professional role in the system, providing comprehensive access to all patient care functions, medical records, prescriptions, and clinical management tools.

Access Level: Full medical and clinical access
Primary Responsibilities: Patient care, medical consultations, prescriptions, clinical documentation
Restrictions: Cannot manage system users or system-wide settings

Permissions & Access

Detailed permissions and access levels for the Doctor role.

Full Access

  • All patient records and medical history
  • Create and edit consultations
  • Create prescriptions (medications & glasses)
  • View financial information
  • Clinical reports and statistics
  • Medical alerts system
  • Personal notes and forum

View Only

  • System-wide statistics (limited)
  • Other doctors' schedules (view only)

Restricted

  • User management (no access)
  • System settings (personal only)
  • Backup and restore (no access)
  • System-wide configuration (no access)
