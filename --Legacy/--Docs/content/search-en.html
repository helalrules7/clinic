Filter Operators

Use special operators to refine your search results with precision.

Filter by Patient/Appointment (&)

Use the & operator to refine your search results:

  • Numeric refinement: Filter by patient ID or appointment ID
    diabetes & 123 - searches for "diabetes" in patient/appointment ID 123
  • Text refinement: Filter by patient name
    medication & Ahmed - searches for "medication" in Ahmed's records

Filter by Date (#)

Use the # symbol to filter results by date:

  • Full date: appointment # 2024-12-25 - searches on specific date
  • Month: prescription # 2024-12 - searches in December 2024
  • Year: patient # 2024 - searches in year 2024

Combined Filters

You can combine both operators for precise searches:

  • diabetes & Ahmed # 2024-12 - searches for "diabetes" in Ahmed's records from December 2024
  • medication & 123 # 2024-12-25 - searches for "medication" in patient/appointment 123 on December 25, 2024
Search Tips:
  • Minimum 2 characters required for search
  • Search is case-insensitive
  • Results are limited to 20 per category for optimal performance
  • Date filter (#) takes precedence over patient filter (&) when both are present

Search Features

The search system includes several powerful features to help you find information quickly and efficiently.

Keyboard Shortcut

Press Ctrl+K (or Cmd+K on Mac) to quickly open the search modal from anywhere in the application.

Top Bar Access

The search bar is prominently displayed in the Top Bar, providing quick access to search functionality at all times.

Categorized Results

Search results are organized by category (appointments, patients, medications, etc.), making it easy to find what you're looking for.

Real-Time Results
