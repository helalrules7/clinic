Auto-Hide Feature

The auto-hide feature automatically conceals the dock when not in use, maximizing your screen space while keeping it easily accessible.

How Auto-Hide Works:
  • Automatic Hiding: When enabled, the dock automatically hides after a period of inactivity, giving you more screen space for your work.
  • Hover to Show: When the dock is hidden, simply move your mouse cursor (or finger on mobile) to the dock's designated area, and it will smoothly reappear.
  • Smart Behavior: The dock remains visible when you're actively using it, only hiding when you're working in other areas of the application.
  • Customizable: You can enable or disable auto-hide through the Settings page to match your preferences.

Focus Mode

Auto-hide helps maintain focus by reducing visual distractions, allowing you to concentrate on your current task.

Maximize Space

Get more screen real estate for your content by automatically hiding the dock when it's not needed.

Quick Access

Even when hidden, the dock is just a hover away, ensuring you never lose quick access to essential features.

Minimize & Expand Features

Control the dock's visibility and explore expanded options for each dock item.

Minimize

Click the minimize icon (eye with slash) to manually collapse the dock. This gives you full control over when the dock is visible, independent of auto-hide settings.

  • Manually hide the dock at any time
  • Restore the dock with a single click
  • Works independently from auto-hide

Expand

Click on any dock icon to expand it and reveal related sub-items and actions. This provides quick access to related features without navigating away.

  • See related sub-items for each dock icon
  • Access additional actions and options
  • Navigate to related features quickly

Expand Menu

Some dock items have expandable menus that show related options. For example, clicking the Documents icon reveals Medical Prescriptions, Glasses Prescriptions, and Media options.

Dock Settings

Control all dock behaviors and preferences through the Settings page.

Settings Page Control:

Navigate to the Settings page to find the "Quick Access Dock" section where you can control all dock-related settings, including visibility and auto-hide behavior.

Show/Hide Dock

The "Quick Access Dock" toggle allows you to show or hide the dock entirely. When enabled (ON), the dock is visible. When disabled (OFF), the dock is completely hidden.

Dock Auto-Hide

The "Dock Auto-Hide" toggle controls whether the dock automatically hides when not in use. When enabled, the dock will hide automatically and show on hover when hidden.

Centralized Control

All dock settings are managed in one convenient location in the Settings page, making it easy to customize the dock behavior to match your workflow.

Settings Options:
  • Quick Access Dock: Toggle to show or hide the dock completely
  • Dock Auto-Hide: Toggle to enable or disable automatic hiding behavior
  • Visual Preview: See a preview of how the dock will look with your current settings
  • Instant Application: Changes are applied immediately when you toggle settings
